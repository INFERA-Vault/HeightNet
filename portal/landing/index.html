02 The method

The image is only the start.

HeightNet keeps the reasoning visible. You get the original RGB, relative depth, calibrated above-ground height, confidence, and the final DSM instead of one unexplained result.

03 The workspace

Four steps.
One place.

Find a location or upload your own image. Then move from map to raster to terrain without leaving the project.

01

Find a scene

Search the world, draw a rectangular area, and choose a Sentinel-2 capture.

02

Read the layers

See relative depth, AGL, ground elevation, and confidence side by side.

03

Calibrate the result

SURE-Cal adjusts the height signal using scene context and a coarse DEM.

04

Walk the terrain

Orbit or fly through the textured mesh, then export or open the layers in QGIS.

04 What you get

Not just a pretty mesh.

The visual scene is the last step. Underneath it are georeferenced products you can download, compare, validate, and carry into QGIS.

  • Relative depthstructural signal from the model
  • Calibrated AGLabove-ground height after SURE-Cal
  • DSM GeoTIFFground elevation plus calibrated height
  • Terrain meshOBJ, MTL, and texture for 3D
